CORE GAMEPLAY

What's Inside Perimeter

Defend your core against relentless waves, automate resource supply lines, and craft powerful gear to conquer hostile sectors.

Sector Defense

9 Sectors & Endless Waves

Push through 9 increasingly difficult sectors, each with tougher enemies and bigger bosses. Survive as long as you can!

Automation

Industrial Factory Automation

Place conveyor belts, build drills, furnaces, and assemblers. Construct 39 distinct factory structures across multiple tiers to craft advanced components and power your defenses.

Research Lab

30+ Upgrades & Lab Research

Spend scrap on permanent tower upgrades for damage, health, and range. Research advanced industrial tech in the lab using factory components to unlock game-changing bonuses and super weapon upgrades.

Equipment

Legendary Gear & Unique Skills

Equip items across 4 gear slots (Weapon, Armor, Relic, Enhancer), each with unique combat skills — shockwaves, chain lightning, healing pulses, and more.

Deckbuilding

Tactical Power Cards

Unlock up to 6 active card slots and equip powerful tactical cards from a collection of 10 to boost your attack speed, damage, and shields.

Visuals & QoL

Dynamic Lighting & Skins

Unlock tower skins with custom explosion effects and particle trails. Play in No-GUI mode for a clean look, or enable Battery Saver to keep your phone cool during long runs.
